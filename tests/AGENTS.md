# tests

Unit tests (`pytest`, no network, no database server). DuckDB tests use `StravaDB(":memory:")`; Postgres-backed code is tested through SQLite or with fakes.

- `test_metrics.py`: load model (CTL/ATL/TSB/ACWR, and the training status's `acwr_ready` waiting for 28 days of history, on a warehouse in a temp file), VO2 max estimator pieces (steady-state mask, grade, Daniels VDOT, best efforts, Riegel), pace zones, planned-load projection.
- `test_pipeline.py`: TRIMP, decoupling, stream upserts, the incremental effectiveness pass, heart-rate zones, best efforts, and `ingest_data` with a fake Strava manager.
- `test_plan_builder.py`: the plan generator: every day covered to race day, long runs on the chosen day, ramp/cutback/taper of weekly volume, quality sessions, metric output, quality session descriptions (warm-up, work and cool-down adding up to the planned distance in miles and km, reps and the tempo block following the distance), a day too short for its session written as an easy run with strides, and every full week before race week adding up to its target at low volumes in miles and km.
- `test_importers.py`: FIT and GPX parsing: the `fixtures/sample_run.fit` fixture (summary, local time from the watch's offset, streams, laps), FIT records without a session, sport mapping and names, GPX tracks and time offsets, refused files, the activity id range.
- `test_imports.py`: the import service on an in-memory warehouse (activity, streams and analysis written, a `dim_athlete` row for a file-only user, an imported track session's reps detected), skipping repeats and runs already synced, moving a file-only user's rows (with structure and reps) to their Strava id, and the `User.athlete_id` resolver.
- `test_warehouse_maintenance.py`: every `activity_id`-keyed table is in `src.pipeline.ACTIVITY_CHILD_TABLES`, deleting an activity with children, deleting an activity and an account's warehouse rows while a second StravaDB holds the warehouse open as the app does (it must not keep a read transaction open, or DuckDB refuses the parent row), and the warehouse rebuild script copying every table.
- `test_sync.py`: the background job registry and its progress snapshots.
- `test_intervals.py`: the interval detector on synthetic streams (track reps, steady run, tempo, a short fast block, rolling hills at even effort, auto-pauses, standing rests, missing readings) and the warehouse pass, with what it reports for a run it has not looked at yet and for activities it never looks at.
- `test_plan_match.py`: marking planned sessions done from synced runs, with SQLite in place of Postgres and an in-memory warehouse.
- `test_energy.py`: the energy model's calibration targets (a normal or good night after an easy day refills to 80 to 95, a 60-TRIMP run costs 10 to 15 and a 150-TRIMP session 30 to 40, a bad night refills at least 15 less), monotonic recharge and drain, clamping to 0 and 100, missing readings and short histories, measured sleep, an early run ending the night, baselines without the judged night, the hourly series and the reasons.
- `test_readiness.py`: the call's level thresholds and trend step, the session hint, and `energy_for` on an in-memory warehouse (baselines from earlier nights only, drains from activity TRIMP, a stale reading keeping the curve but no call).
- `test_app.py`: the route table, so a router that fails to mount or a removed endpoint that returns fails CI, `/api/energy` refusing another user's id, and `/api/activities/{id}/intervals` telling a run not analysed yet from one with nothing found.
- `test_account.py`: account deletion with SQLite in place of Postgres and an in-memory warehouse: every `athlete_id`-keyed table is one the service clears, two users with a row in every table (feedback notes among them) and only the leaving one's rows gone, a wrong password deleting nothing, the Strava revoke attempted (and its failure ignored) only for a user with a token, and the route refusing during a sync, mapping the wrong password to 400 and clearing the session.
- `test_admin.py`: the admin page's numbers from plain rows (weeks Monday to Sunday, the seventh day counting as back, accounts without a sign-up date in the totals only), those rows read from SQLite and an in-memory warehouse, the last-seen stamp written at most once a day, the Feedback endpoint (stored with its page and contact choice, blank refused with 400, too long with 422, 401 logged out), and the admin gate: a logged-in user in `ADMIN_EMAILS` gets the page, its link and its API, and everyone else a 404.
- `test_demo.py`: the demo's seeding (user, race, plan from the fake history's volume) against SQLite, and that seeding again replaces only the demo rows.
- `test_fakes.py`: the fake Strava and COROS the browser tests and the demo use: the default runs unchanged, the varied history's span, kinds and streams agreeing with summaries, mile laps, paging, nights.
- `test_email.py`: `send_email` over a stand-in SMTP class: the HTML as the text's alternative, and a text-only message without it.
- `test_digest.py`: when the weekly digest is due, the email text (with the morning's energy and today's call), its HTML version (the same numbers, names typed by users escaped, and no empty sections without data), the plan line counting a session done when the dashboard does (a warehouse in a temp file and SQLite), and a scheduler pass sending once and stamping the user (SQLite in place of Postgres).
- `test_pacing.py`: clock parsing, goals read for the distance (a two-part time as m:ss or h:mm, goals outside 3:30 to 20:00/mi refused), even and negative splits summing to the goal, whole-second splits (a full split's pace equals its split time, a short last split keeps the planned pace, the clock stays on the plan, a negative split never slows down), the grade cost curve, a hilly profile slowing the climbs, GPX parsing of an inline course, the stretched-course warning, rejected files, the prediction the goal falls back to, and, with SQLite in place of Postgres, the pacing endpoint's goal sources and refusals, the predictions card reading race goals the same way, and the course library routes (courses of the race distance by name without file paths, a chosen course stored as an upload is and a bad one refused with the old course kept, 503 when the library cannot be read).
- `test_course_library.py`: the race-courses reader with `requests.get` answered from a dict of files: the index read once a day and only its published courses kept, the copy in memory kept when a later read fails, an index that is not a list of races refused, only files the index lists fetched, a file over 5 MB refused, and `attach` storing a course or leaving the race without one (no file, not a course, library offline).
- `test_courses.py`: the public course pages logged out with the library stubbed: the course list, a plan for any goal at a course's own distance, the refusals (no course file, unknown course, implausible goal, bad strategy or unit, 503 without the library), the home page, list and course page rendered server-side (each card and row saying its event, with the check note and the OpenStreetMap credit), and the sitemap and robots.txt.
- `test_races.py`: the race catalog (planned distances only, biggest first, `has_course`, 503 when the library cannot be read), races created from it with their course or without one (none in the dataset, library offline), and the first race at sign-up named and given its course, with SQLite in place of Postgres.
- `test_coros.py`: the COROS client against a fake HTTP session (login hashing, chunked daily fetch, auth and rate-limit errors), and the sync into an in-memory warehouse: nights stored, resting heart rate default and recompute trigger, expired sessions, unlink.
- `test_webhook.py`, `test_password_reset.py`: the webhook dispatcher, reset tokens and the reset email (the same link in its text and HTML).

`conftest.py` has `make_gpx`, a factory for GPX bytes of a straight run with heart rate, at a steady pace or in `(seconds, speed)` blocks. `fixtures/sample_run.fit` is a ten-minute, two-lap run written by `fixtures/make_fit_fixture.py` (a minimal FIT encoder; run it only to change the fixture).

Browser tests live in `../e2e/`.
