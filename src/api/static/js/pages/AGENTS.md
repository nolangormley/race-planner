# pages

One module per template. Each reads its ids from `config` in `app.js`, fetches the page's data, renders into the template's placeholders, and listens for `strava:synced` to reload.

- `dashboard.js`: loads each card's data into one `state` object and hands it to the renderers below; also the header with the next race, the Connect-or-import card (in the template for a user without Strava, shown only while they have no activities), the unit toggle and the fitness chart's range buttons.
  Renderers take what they draw as arguments (`state`, or one endpoint's data) and each module keeps its own chart instances.
- `dashboard-training.js`: the fitness, fatigue (its acute:chronic ratio only once the server's `acwr_ready` says the history covers four weeks; before that the foot says so), form and VO2 max tiles from `/status` and `/vo2max`, the fitness/form panel chart (forward along the saved plan from `/api/projection`, or the server's forecast), weekly volume, heart-rate zones and time by type from `/analytics`, and the aerobic efficiency chart from `/api/efficiency`.
- `dashboard-tables.js`: race predictions from `/api/predictions`, personal records from `/api/records`, pace zones, this week's plan next to the runs (a planned Intervals or Tempo day whose analysed runs have another structure shows "Ran, not the session"; today's session carries the readiness hint), and recent activities.
- `dashboard-recovery.js`: the recovery card below its energy block: HRV and resting heart rate tiles and chart from `/api/recovery`, and today's readiness badge from `/api/readiness`. With COROS switched off the template leaves the card out and none of these is fetched; a 404 hides the card.
- `dashboard-insights.js`: the What to work on card under the tiles: the notes from `/api/insights` (badge with the label, title, text, evidence), an empty state, and the card hidden when the request fails. `dashboard.js` fetches it again on a unit switch, since the notes carry paces.
- `energy.js`: not a page of its own; `dashboard.js` calls its `renderEnergy` with `/api/energy` for the top of the recovery card: the level now, this morning's level against the usual one, the reasons, and the last week hour by hour as an area panel with the nights as `xBands` and a marker at every activity (labels on the largest drains that have room at the current width), and the Day by day table under it from the response's `ledger`.
- `streams.js`: an activity's summary strip, stream panels with pace-zone shading from `/pace_zones` and the detected reps shaded as `xBands`, the Detected structure card (summary line and reps table from `/api/activities/{id}/intervals`; without reps one line says nothing was found or the run is not analysed yet, and an activity the pass never looks at gets no card), laps, effort metrics and time in zones from `/effectiveness`.
  A run shows pace and steps per minute; any other type shows speed (laps compare in the same unit) and cadence as it comes.
- `activities.js`: the sortable activity table with a name search and the type filter, 50 rows at a time behind **Show more** (the count covers every match), delete, **Import files** (posts the chosen FIT/GPX files to `/api/activities/import` as `FormData`, toasts the per-file results, reloads the list), and the workout library CRUD behind the second tab.
- `calendar.js`: the month grid of plan entries and Strava activities, the add/edit modal with attach-to-activity, the month summary meter and the upcoming list. The Build plan modal lists upcoming races of a supported distance, prefills weekly distance from the last four weeks in `/analytics`, and posts to `/api/training-plan/{id}/build`. Planned distances show through `units.planned` from `app.js` (in the chips, the upcoming list and the modal's Distance field, whose placeholder follows the unit too), and the month summary adds them up with `plannedMeters`.
- `profile.js`: athlete details edit, password change, unit and theme preferences, the weekly digest switch with preview (the HTML email in a sandboxed frame without scripts, grown to its height) and send-now (`/api/digest/{id}`), races CRUD (the name field is a race search from `../race-search.js`: picking a known race sets the distance and sends its catalog `course`, and the row says "course map" when the race has one), Strava status with last sync, the COROS card (connect form, status, sync now, reconnect, unlink via `/api/coros/{id}`), warehouse totals. Each race row links to its pacing page.
  Edit opens once `/api/hr-profile` has answered, and the save sends a heart rate only when it changed: a null resets it and recomputes every activity.
- `profile-account.js`: `initDeleteAccount`, called by `profile.js`: the Delete account modal asks for the password, calls `DELETE /api/account`, shows a refusal in the form's status line, and on success sends the browser to `/login?deleted=1`.
- `home.js`: the home page for visitors: the race search over `/api/courses` opens the course page of the race picked.
- `courses.js`: the course list's filter: rows whose race, city or state lack a typed word hide.
- `course.js`: the public course page: an example goal at 9:00 a mile on load, then the goal, strategy and unit controls over `/api/courses/{race}/{event}/pacing`, drawn by `../pacing-view.js`, and the printable pace band.
- `setup.js`: the first race at sign-up: the race search on `#race_name` sets the distance and the hidden `course_race`/`course_event` fields the form posts.
- `pacing.js`: one race's pacing plan from `/api/races/{id}/pacing`, drawn by `../pacing-view.js`: goal input, even/negative strategy and unit toggles, GPX upload/remove, the course profile as an area panel with split markers, the splits table, and the pace band that the print stylesheet shows on its own.
  **Find course** opens the course library modal: the courses of the race's distance from `/api/races/{id}/course-library` (loaded once a page), a search where every typed word must appear in the race name, event, city or state, a **Check it** badge with the reason on `warn` courses, and **Use**, which posts to `/api/races/{id}/course/library` and reloads the plan.
- `admin.js`: the admin page from `/api/admin/stats`: the sign-up cohorts table (eight weeks and an All time row, with a line for accounts from before sign-up dates were kept) and the latest feedback notes.
