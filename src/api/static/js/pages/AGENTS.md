# pages

One module per template. Each reads its ids from `config` in `app.js`, fetches the page's data, renders into the template's placeholders, and listens for `strava:synced` to reload.

- `dashboard.js`: status tiles from `/status`, the fitness/form panel chart, weekly volume, heart-rate zones and time-by-type from `/analytics`, VO2 max from `/vo2max`, race predictions from `/api/predictions`, the aerobic efficiency chart from `/api/efficiency`, the recovery card from `/api/recovery` with today's readiness badge from `/api/readiness` (its hint also shows under today's planned session; with COROS switched off the template leaves the card out and neither is fetched, and a 404 hides the card), pace zones, this week's plan next to actual activities (a planned Intervals or Tempo day whose analysed runs have another structure shows "Ran, not the session"), recent activities, the coach's note, and the unit toggle.
- `streams.js`: an activity's summary strip, stream panels with pace-zone shading from `/pace_zones` and the detected reps shaded as `xBands`, the Detected structure card (summary line and reps table from `/api/activities/{id}/intervals`), laps, effort metrics and time in zones from `/effectiveness`, optional AI note.
- `activities.js`: the sortable, filterable activity table with delete, and the workout library CRUD behind the second tab.
- `calendar.js`: the month grid of plan entries and Strava activities, the add/edit modal with attach-to-activity, the month summary meter and the upcoming list. The Build plan modal lists upcoming races of a supported distance, prefills weekly distance from the last four weeks in `/analytics`, and posts to `/api/training-plan/{id}/build`. `plannedMeters` parses free-text planned distances.
- `profile.js`: athlete details edit, password change, unit and theme preferences, the weekly digest switch with preview and send-now (`/api/digest/{id}`), races CRUD, Strava status with last sync, the COROS card (connect form, status, sync now, reconnect, unlink via `/api/coros/{id}`), warehouse totals. Each race row links to its pacing page.
- `pacing.js`: one race's pacing plan from `/api/races/{id}/pacing`: goal input, even/negative strategy and unit toggles, GPX upload/remove, the course profile as an area panel with split markers, the splits table, and the pace band that the print stylesheet shows on its own.
